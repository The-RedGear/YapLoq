# YapLoq 0.6.4

Add sound effects to your messages and replay whole conversations from one queue entry.

## What's new

- **Audio Stims.** Use tags such as `[Burp]` to play a sound between spoken lines. Pick a single file or build a weighted sound library, with volume controls, previews and a separate audio output for each keyword.
- **A familiar sound editor.** Keywords have their own icons and colors, with searchable lists and popup editors like voice aliases. Keywords can also replace censored words or pause while a Streamer.bot action supplies the sound.
- **Grouped queue replay.** Expand a message to see its voices, sounds and pauses in order, or select the group to replay it from the beginning. The group keeps the first voice's icon and color.
- **Speech prepared ahead.** Upcoming speech generates in the background instead of waiting for playback to begin, reducing gaps between clips.
- **Sound-linked Streamer.bot actions.** Run an action when a sound starts, with the original viewer information when supplied. Previews never run actions.

## Fixes and improvements

- Sounds replay from their original files without adding copies to the audio cache.
- Queue-finished events wait for the final sound. Sound-only messages do not start speech/avatar hooks.
- Clearer sound-library controls, themed playlist checkboxes and improved custom icon loading.
- Sound names are no longer incorrectly masked in the queue, and the profanity picker shows their icons and colors.
- Canceling a sound editor leaves its original settings unchanged.

## Update

Use the download icon beside the version number, or download
**YapLoq-Windows-x64-update.zip** and extract the complete folder.

Keep your sound files in their original locations so they remain available for playback and replay.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Keep `_internal` beside `YapLoq.exe`. No Python installation
is required. The app is unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
Cloud speech services may charge for usage.
